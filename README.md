# Financial Institutions – Holistic Strategy Research

Comprehensive strategic research across 21 financial institutions covering full business, technology, operating model, partnerships, and opportunity landscape.

**Prepared by: Kumar Raju**

## Scope
This research goes beyond digital assets to cover each firm's complete strategic picture:
- Business segments and financial performance
- Technology strategy (AI, cloud, data, platforms, digital assets)
- Operating model and transformation programmes
- People and leadership
- Vendor/partner ecosystem
- Opportunities, risks, and skills gaps
- Product and partnership opportunities
- Analyst judgment with watchlist items
